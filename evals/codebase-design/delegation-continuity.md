# Delegated rights follow their grantors

Fictional mini-repository specification. No fixture execution or real service is implied.

## Prompt and fixture

Prompt: "Finish the shared-account representative flow in shop-api."

1. A delegation recorded at time 10 was granted by people A and B. Holdings carry person identity,
   effective bounds and recording time. The current rule checks only revocation and uses different
   ownership projections in preparation and use.
2. Supply separate histories for B leaving, C arriving, A and B exchanging shares, B leaving then
   returning, C recorded at 15 with effect at 5, and a partially recorded allocation.
3. An older representation document is available as fallback. The fictional contract requires
   continuous membership of the original granting people and complete allocation evidence.

## Acceptance and admitted controls

Use one ownership projection and coverage rule across preparation, use, equivalent evidence and
server verdicts. The internal share exchange remains valid; departure, arrival, departure then
return and incomplete evidence refuse. Late backdating cannot add a grantor to the set known at
recording. Check every effective change point through use, not just the endpoints. A lapsed
delegation refuses without falling back to the older document. Verify each history separately,
including unchanged people represented by different holding rows.

In disposable copies, restore endpoint-only comparison, row-identity comparison and silent fallback
one at a time. Each must fail its named assertion; the restored rule must pass.

## Comparison protocol

Freeze acceptance and controls before trials. Give two fresh sessions identical disposable copies
of the fictional fixture and prompt, without the acceptance section. Load the base and candidate
versions of the inputs listed in the paired results file. Use neutral variant labels and one blind
judge; record skill loading separately from application. Retain fixture and input fingerprints,
model, host, commands, outputs and restored controls. A gain requires observed failure without the
addition and success with it. Repository checks do not execute this scenario.
