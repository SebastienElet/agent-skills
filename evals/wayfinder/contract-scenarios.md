# Wayfinder interview scenarios

These are expected behaviors, not recorded executions.

| Phase              | Situation                                                                     | Expected result                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Initial charting   | A destination needs two dependent product choices and one independent choice. | Clarify the destination, map all three questions and their dependencies, and stop without resolving the branches. Retain imprecise uncertainty separately.                        |
| Resume             | A frontier choice is selected while another choice remains open.              | Interview the selected choice until answered or blocked; record its outcome and retain the other question and updated frontier. Do not resolve a second choice in the invocation. |
| Resume, unanswered | The selected choice needs a user answer that is not supplied.                 | Leave it blocked and preserve the other questions; neither silence nor a recommendation becomes an answer.                                                                        |
